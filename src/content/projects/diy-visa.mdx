## The solution

DIY Visa grew from firsthand experience with confusing DS-160 questions. Applicants work section by section, review their own answers, catch possible inconsistencies, and practice a short interview in English or Bahasa Indonesia.

## What changed

The hackathon MVP supports multiple private preparation cases, conditional questions, five-second autosave, human-readable review, photo guidance, and three-question text or voice interviews. Supporting tools include consent-based document auditing and optional retained files.

## Behind the solution

Next.js server actions and route handlers call typed capability services. Supabase handles authentication and PostgreSQL storage through Drizzle. The Vercel AI SDK connects to an OpenAI-compatible provider, while private Vercel Blob stores explicitly retained documents.

### Technical decisions

- Encrypt sensitive identity and financial fields before storing them in PostgreSQL.
- Enforce case ownership and tenant isolation on the server.
- Require consent for AI processing and confirmation before extracted facts update an application.
- Keep uploads transient by default and make retained-file deletion explicit.
- Keep model output schema-validated and expose uncertainty rather than approval predictions.

### Key features

- Guided DS-160 preparation with conditional questions and autosave.
- Missing-answer, timeline, typo, and consistency review.
- Photo guidance and document review with traveler confirmation.
- Bilingual text or voice interview practice.
- Private document organization and a rolling retention policy.

## Lessons learned

Trust needs to exist in storage rules, permission checks, interface copy, and user control. A focused preparation tool is more useful than trying to automate the entire government process.

## Product boundaries

DIY Visa supports preparation only. It does not submit the DS-160, pay fees, book appointments, provide legal advice, or predict approval. Official applications remain under the traveler’s control on authorized government channels. The product is independent of the U.S. government.

## AI system implementation

Typed server-side capability services use the Vercel AI SDK with an OpenAI-compatible provider for answer review, consent-based document auditing, and bilingual interview practice. Model output is schema-validated; extracted facts require traveler confirmation before updating a case. Ownership checks and encrypted sensitive fields protect stored case data. AI supports preparation, not submission, legal advice, or approval prediction.
